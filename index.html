Story

A fonda with fine-dining roots

Fonda Del Barrio opened in August 2025 at 2234 Logan Avenue, in the Chicano heritage-rich Barrio Logan district. Torres, Executive Chef Eduardo Baeza and Sous Chef Jesus Rodriguez build the menu from Mexico's seven primary culinary regions — North, North Pacific Coast, Bajío, Central, South Pacific Coast, Gulf Coast and Yucatán — with café de olla brewed the traditional way, in a clay pot.

  • ✓

    Seven regions on one menu — from Oaxacan mole to Yucatán-style octopus.

  • ✓

    Mezcal & tequila bar — cocktail program by Rudy Corona, plus Mexican wines and beers.

  • ✓

    Live music Thu–Sun — on the bar/lounge side of the 90-seat room.
